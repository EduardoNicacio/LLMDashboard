Testing Observations

Key findings and notes from the Qwen3.6 vs Claude Opus comparison.

Local Inference Performance:

The RTX 3090 can sustain ~28k tokens/sec with Qwen3.6, which is impressive for a local setup. However, this comes at the cost of high GPU utilization (75-85%) and significant power draw.

Quality Tradeoffs:

While Claude Opus maintains slightly better quality scores, Qwen3.6 shows competitive results with the advantage of being completely private and cost-free for inference.

Coding Performance:

The 4% coding accuracy gap between models is notable but may not be significant for most use cases. Both models handle basic algorithms well, with Claude showing slightly better optimization suggestions.

Recommendations:
  • For development/testing: Use Qwen3.6 locally for fast iterations
  • For production: Consider Claude Opus for higher reliability
  • Monitor GPU temperatures during extended local inference sessions
